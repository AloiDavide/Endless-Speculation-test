## Corollaries

There is one hint hidden away in Banquet that makes this riddle trivial to solve, while Kyrie is fighting one of the sevens stakes, she mentions that she and Battler's mother gave birth on the same day and that her son died.
With this red truth we can come to the conclusion that Asumu was actually the one who had a miscarriage.
